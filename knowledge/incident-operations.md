# Klaxon incident operations

## Alert grouping

Normalize source, service, and signal before computing the grouping fingerprint. Preserve occurrence count, first seen, last seen, and source payload references for audit.

## Escalation

Resolve the effective on-call target when a step becomes due. Respect schedule overrides and record queued, sent, delivered, or failed status for every channel attempt.

## Command

Assign one incident commander. The timeline separates observations, actions, status changes, and notifications so a later review can reconstruct what happened.

## Handoffs

Confirm coverage before ending a shift. Include active incidents, unacknowledged alerts, and temporary overrides in the handoff summary.
